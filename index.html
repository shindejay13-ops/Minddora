Everyday Wellness

Small reminders for everyday mental wellbeing.

🌿 Manage stress

Break a large task into smaller steps. Take short breaks and ask for support when needed.

💜 Understand emotions

All emotions are valid. Naming what you feel can help you reflect before responding.

🌙 Support your sleep

Try a consistent sleep routine and reduce screen use before bedtime.

☀️ Practice self-care

Water, nourishing food, movement, rest and connection can all support wellbeing.

Need Support?

If you feel overwhelmed, speak with someone you trust or contact a qualified mental health professional. If you may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or go to the nearest emergency department.

Mindora is not monitored and cannot provide emergency assistance.
