2 & 7. Guided Breathing & Sound

Relaxation Corner

Follow the 4-4-4 breathing rhythm and play soothing ambient frequencies.

What is Guided Breathing?
A simple breathing practice that may help you slow down and settle when you feel tense. How to do it: breathe in gently for 4 counts, hold comfortably for 4 counts, then breathe out gently for 4 counts. Repeat at your own comfortable pace. Do not force or hold your breath if it feels uncomfortable.
Relaxation Sound: choose Calm or Sleep to hear a soft tone; press Stop whenever you want. Keep the volume low.

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Exercise

Bring your focus to now

Feeling worried, stressed, or overwhelmed? Take a slow breath and use your five senses to notice the world around you. There is no right or wrong answer.

👀 5 things you can SEE

Look around and name five things you can see.

✋ 4 things you can TOUCH

Notice four things you can touch and how they feel.

👂 3 things you can HEAR

Pause and listen for three different sounds.

👃 2 things you can SMELL

Notice two smells around you. If you cannot smell anything, think of two familiar pleasant smells.

👅 1 thing you can TASTE

Notice the taste in your mouth, or take a sip of water and notice its taste.

Take your time. Breathe gently. You can leave any step blank if it is difficult.

3. Stress Management Centre

Student Focus

Turn overwhelming study pressure into one clear, achievable micro-step.

What is Stress Management?
It helps you break a large or stressful task into a small step that feels manageable. How to use it: type one task you can do now (for example, revise 5 pages), tap Add Priority Step, and tick it when finished. Start with one step instead of worrying about everything at once.

4. Burnout Warning-Sign Checklist

Balance Tracker

Check any warning signs you noticed this week:

What is the Burnout Warning-Sign Checklist?
Burnout can happen when ongoing pressure leaves you exhausted or disconnected. This checklist is a reflection tool, not a diagnosis. How to use it: select the signs you have noticed this week, then tap Check Burnout Load. Use the feedback as a reminder to rest, eat, hydrate, take breaks, and seek support if difficulties continue.
Difficulty concentrating or retaining study material
Feeling emotionally drained even after sleeping
Skipping meals, water breaks, or fresh air
Irritability or feeling detached from friends/family

Educational Self-Assessment

PSS / GAD-7 Inspired
What is Self-Assessment?
This short educational check-in helps you reflect on recent stress and worry. It is not a medical test or diagnosis. Answer honestly based on how you have felt over the last 7 days, choose the closest option for each question, and tap View Educational Feedback. If your feelings are intense or affecting daily life, consider speaking with a trusted adult or qualified counsellor.

Over the last 7 days, how often have you felt nervous, anxious, or unable to stop worrying?

How often have you felt difficulties were piling up so high you could not overcome them?

*Note: This educational screening tool promotes self-awareness and is not a clinical medical diagnosis.

What Would You Do?

Psychology Game
What is the Scenario Game?
It gives you a real-life style situation to practise thoughtful responses. Read the situation, choose the response you would most likely take, and review the feedback. The aim is to pause, consider different explanations, and respond calmly—not to get a perfect score.

Scenario: You are stressed before a big exam, and your close friend leaves your message on "Seen" without replying. What do you do?

What is the Study Focus Timer?
It helps you study in focused blocks with planned breaks. How to use it: enter how many minutes you want to study and rest, tap Apply Time Settings, then Start. Use Pause if you need to stop briefly, Reset to begin that session again, or Skip Task to move to the next block. When a block ends, the next block starts automatically.

What is Guided Meditation?
A short quiet pause to notice your breathing and bring attention back to the present. How to use it: sit comfortably, select 1, 5, or 10 minutes, and follow the on-screen reminder. Let thoughts come and go without judging yourself. Tap End Session whenever you wish.

Sleep Relaxation

Wind down

A gentle bedtime routine to slow down before sleep.

What is Sleep Relaxation?
A simple wind-down routine to help you shift from study or screen time toward rest. How to use it: dim your screen, put away study materials, relax your jaw and shoulders, breathe comfortably for a minute, and think of one good thing from today. Tap the completion button when you finish. This routine supports relaxation but does not treat sleep problems.
  1. Dim your screen and put away study materials.
  2. Relax your jaw, shoulders and hands.
  3. Breathe slowly and comfortably for one minute.
  4. Notice one good thing from today.
